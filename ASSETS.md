# Site assets

Brand art (Elysium identity):

- `public/media/brand-header.png`: JellySwarm X header, 1500×500. Used for social previews.
- `public/media/brand-pfp.png`: JellySwarm profile image, 400×400. Used for the logo and favicon.
- `public/media/jelly-*.png`: transparent mascot cutouts. They were lifted with macOS Vision subject masking from the profile image, the X header and the "How to use it" guide video.

Components:

- Meet-the-swarm gallery: Scrolltide Orbit Gallery (`src/components/orbit`), restyled for the Elysium palette. https://www.scrolltide.co/components#c-orbit-gallery
- Start-a-job button: Scrolltide Eye Button (`src/components/eye`), restyled in mint. https://www.scrolltide.co/components#c-eye-button

The earlier candy-coloured mascots, office films and photographs were removed in the Elysium rebrand. They remain in the original developer handoff archive.
