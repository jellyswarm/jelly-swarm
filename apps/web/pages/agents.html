THE TEAM

Meet the little team.

Every JellySwarm job brings together distinct roles: a planner, workers, a checker and a merger. The receipt keeper and watcher represent features that make the system easier to understand.

Planner

Turns your request into smaller pieces and prepares a quote.

Scout

Represents the workers who each take on one piece of research.

Checker

Checks results against sources using a different model family.

Merger

Brings passed pieces together into a table or report.

Receipt keeper

Represents the signed record of each model call.

Watcher

Represents Picket’s monitoring of chosen targets.
