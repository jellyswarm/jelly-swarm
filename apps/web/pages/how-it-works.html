HOW IT WORKS

One big job. Many little steps.

Give JellySwarm a request. The planner proposes a job and a fixed quote before the swarm gets to work.

1. Describe

Write what you need in plain words. The planner breaks the job into pieces.

2. Work

Workers handle their assigned pieces in parallel, up to 247 agents per job.

3. Check

A checker uses a different model family and checks each piece against its sources.

4. Bring it together

Passed pieces are merged into a result with links back to sources.
