Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026. This explains what JellySwarm collects, why, and who it's shared with.

This is a draft written for a service still in development, not a final, lawyer-reviewed policy. Have it reviewed against your actual data flows, your jurisdiction's requirements (e.g. GDPR, CCPA), and a lawyer before relying on it in production.

What we collect, and why

An anonymous device identifier. On your first visit we set a signed, random cookie that identifies this browser — not you by name. It's used to enforce the free-tier limits (one free job per device, ever) and to detect likely self-referral (the same device claiming its own referral code). It is never used for cross-site advertising tracking.

Sign-in identity, if you choose one. JellySwarm can be used anonymously via an access key, or signed into with a passkey, an external wallet, or an email link:

  • Passkeys use public-key cryptography. We store a public key and a credential id — never a password, never a private key.
  • Wallet sign-in stores the wallet address you signed with, nothing else.
  • Email sign-in never stores your email address in plaintext. We store a one-way HMAC of it (a cryptographic fingerprint), which is enough to recognize you on a later sign-in but cannot be reversed back into your address. The address itself is held only long enough to send the sign-in link, then discarded.

Your job's content. The request and item list you submit for a job, and the results produced, are what you're paying us to process — they're stored so you can read your own job back, and sent to the model provider that runs the job (currently OpenRouter and whichever model host it routes to). A checked result's source links point to pages fetched over the open web, which may themselves log the request per their own policies.

Your IP address, briefly. We use your IP address for two abuse-prevention purposes, and don't retain the full address beyond that: a coarse network "prefix" (e.g. the first three octets of an IPv4 address) is checked against recent referral sign-ups from the same network, and against the free tier's per-network daily limit. For the latter, your IP is sent to RIPEstat, a public lookup run by RIPE NCC (a regional internet registry), solely to ask which network operator it belongs to — no other data about you is sent with that request.

Credits and referrals. If you refer someone or are referred, we record the relationship and the resulting credit balance against your account. Credits expire 90 days after being issued if unused.

What we never collect

No passwords (there is no password-based sign-in at all). No government ID or identity documents. No plaintext email addresses, ever, as described above.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the product: the model-inference provider that runs your job, an email provider if you use email sign-in, and the public IP-lookup service described above. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Money and wallets

We never ask for or hold your wallet's private key or seed phrase. Payments happen through your own wallet signing a transaction, or through a non-custodial on-chain escrow contract — funds sit in that contract, not in a company account, until the work is checked and settled.

Your choices

You can use JellySwarm anonymously without ever signing in. If you've signed in and want your identity data removed or exported, contact us at the address below — job records tied to an active or disputed job may need to be retained until that job's dispute window closes.

Children

JellySwarm is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under 16.
