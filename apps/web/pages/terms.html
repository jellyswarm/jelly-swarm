What the service is

JellySwarm is an engine that splits a request across up to 247 AI agents, has an independent checker verify each result against its sources, and gives you one fixed price before any of it runs. Fanout is the general-purpose door to that engine; Trawl applies it to researching lists (tokens, wallets, or everyday items); Picket applies it to ongoing monitoring of chosen targets. Your first job is free, subject to the limits below; later jobs are paid for through a connected wallet, a prepaid balance, or a credit balance.

Not financial advice

Nothing JellySwarm produces is investment, legal, or financial advice, and none of its products place trades or move your funds on your behalf — Picket watches and reports; it never executes anything. Checked results reduce, but do not eliminate, the chance of an error. Verify anything you intend to rely on for a financial decision.

Pricing, payment, and refunds

The price for a job is fixed and shown before it runs. Rows that fail independent checking are refunded automatically through the on-chain escrow contract, not by a support request. Jobs can be paid for by a wallet signature, a prepaid vault balance under a spend grant you control, a studio-sponsored free-tier slot (capped in size, and limited per device, network, and account to prevent abuse), or your own credit balance (e.g. from a referral), which is only ever charged for what a job actually settles for.

Transactions on a public blockchain are generally irreversible, network fees ("gas") apply and fluctuate outside our control, and software — audited or not — can contain bugs. We will never ask you for your wallet's seed phrase or private key; anyone who does, claiming to be us, is not us. You are responsible for the security of your own wallet.

The free tier is rate-limited per device, per network, and (where we can determine it) per hosting network, and gated by a small proof-of-work challenge your browser solves automatically. A device or account that repeatedly submits requests we refuse may have its limits tightened, and may eventually be blocked from starting new free or paid jobs. A ban never blocks a withdrawal or a refund you're already owed — it only stops new jobs from starting.
