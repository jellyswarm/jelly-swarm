JELLYSWARM: Overview

Up to 247 open-model agents on one job. You see a fixed price before it runs, your
money waits in escrow while it runs, and you pay only for the work that passes.

One engine, three products: FANOUT ($FNOUT)**, **TRAWL ($TRWL) and PICKET
($PIKT). Jobs settle on Base, Solana and EVM network, plus HyperEVM / Elysium
for Picket. Prepared 2026-11-07. (U) marks a claim we have not verified independently.

In plain words

An agent swarm is a team of AIs that splits one big job between them:

1. A planner reads your request and cuts it into small pieces.
2. Up to 247 workers each take one piece, all at the same time.
3. A checker re-checks every piece against its sources. It comes from a different AI

     family from the workers, so it doesn't share their blind spots.
4. A merger stitches the passed pieces into one table or report.

Work that would take one AI an hour takes the swarm minutes. Swarms suit wide work:
"here are 300 tokens; give me holders, liquidity lock and next unlocks for each, with a
link behind every cell."

What 247 means. It is the most agents JellySwarm will ever put on one job. It is a hard
cap, and every quote shows it ("this job will use 183 of 247 agents"). Longer lists run in
waves. It counts agents. It is not shorthand for "all day, every day". When a service
never stops, we say "around the clock". A full-width job is about 250 model calls, and
our prices are built on that unit.

One engine, three products. Fanout is the engine's public door. Trawl and Picket are
apps that run on Fanout's own API, so their jobs leave the same public receipts.

2. Your first small job is free. You don't need a wallet. Sign in with a passkey or an

4. Get your table, with a source behind every cell. Every result has its own link, which
     you can keep private or make public, plus a Run this again button.
5. Keep going.

first. Worker pools scale across several model hosts, and Open-tier jobs can also use
community GPUs. Busy hours mean a longer wait, never a higher price.

How the tokens work

Each product has its own token, venue and fee stream, so one product's bad week can't
drain the others.

Where each payment goes. Every paid row splits 80% / 10% / 5% / 5%:

     80% to worker operators
     10% to the treasury
     5% to checker pay
     5% to buy-and-burn of that product's token

Burns are funded only by outside stablecoin fees. The burn share spends only USDC
or USDG paid for jobs by wallets outside our own set. Each burn transaction links to the
job receipts that funded it. When we, or wallets linked to us, pay for a job, that share
goes to the treasury instead. Free jobs and referral credits never fund burns.

A public meter for real paid volume. /stats counts a job as real only if all of these
are true:

     an outside wallet paid for it;
     that wallet was not funded by one of ours within 3 hops over the past 30 days;
     a checker separate from the workers checked it;
     it was worth at least $1.

What a token is for:

     Access: higher caps and priority in the queue.
     Credits: a monthly allowance of free small jobs, paid from the treasury.
     Discounts: up to 20% off quotes.
     Checker stake: checkers stake the token to sit on a panel and lose part of the stake
     if they approve bad work.

What a token never does. It never pays for jobs, which are always priced in USDC or
USDG. It carries no trading tax and no emissions, and its supply never changes after
launch. Checker pay is a wage for checking work, not income for holders.

What it won't do

     Trade, place orders or hold keys. Agent trading still loses money. Olas'
     Polymarket agents averaged −4.14% ROI (Olas).
     Hold your money outside escrow or your own wallet. Your embedded wallet's
     key stays on your device. The owner can withdraw a prepaid balance at any time,
     even while the system is halted.
     Ask for ID, a card or a company. There is no KYC or KYB. Email is optional and
     used only to sign in.
     Buy, book, claim or send. Everyday mode compares options but doesn't buy.
     Wallet sweeps are read-only. Lead lists are delivered to you, never sent.
     Give advice, or treat a guess as a fact. Results have no buy or sell column and
     name no private person. A cell the checker can't verify shows "unverified".
     Run abusive jobs. It never scrapes behind a login or ignores robots.txt, and it
     rejects spam, outreach and flood-shaped jobs.

Honest limits

     We sign the receipts on Open and Standard jobs. They prove what we say ran,
     not what the hardware did. Only Sealed jobs carry hardware attestation.
     We run the checkers and workers at launch. Until outside checkers stake in,
     "independent" means a separate process, key and model family, not a separate
     organisation. Until outside operators join, the 80% goes to our own operator wallets.

FAQ

Does every job get 247 agents?
No. 247 is the ceiling, and your quote shows the exact number your job will use.

What if a job fails halfway?
You pay for the rows that passed. Rows that fail twice are refunded at settlement. If
nothing passes, everything is refunded. If a job never settles, anyone can trigger a full
refund once it expires.

Do I need the token?
No. You pay in USDC or USDG. The token gives you discounts, a small free monthly
allowance and priority in the queue.

Can my AI pay on its own?
Yes, over x402, if your AI holds a wallet. If it doesn't, top up a prepaid balance once, buy
a wallet-bound API key and give your AI the key. The key can spend only up to the caps
you set.

Who sees my data?
Each worker sees only its own piece. Wallet lists run only on TEE hosts, never on
community GPUs. Shared links and public transcripts show only what you choose to
publish. Wallet sweeps can't be made public.

What if I lose my phone?
Your wallet key is a passkey, so it syncs through your own device account. You can also
add a second passkey or a backup wallet address. We can't recover it for you, because
we never hold it.

Can I run a node?
Yes, but only for Open-tier worker calls:

     Serve gpt-oss or Qwen 3.x on vLLM.
     Prove your model with a GPU-TEE quote or a fingerprint challenge.
     Stake USDC plus the product's token.

We re-run 5% of calls on a trusted host. A mismatch costs 10% of your stake. You're
paid in USDC from the worker share of passed rows, never in emissions.
